# Molecularly Imprinted Polymer (MIP) Synthesis Protocol

To ensure the QCM sensor only reacts to Alpha-Terpinyl Acetate (ATA), an MIP coating was synthesized using the following steps:

## Reagents
* **Template:** Alpha-terpinyl acetate (ATA)
* **Functional Monomer:** Methacrylic acid (MAA)
* **Cross-linker:** Ethylene glycol dimethacrylate (EGDMA)
* **Initiator:** Azobisisobutyronitrile (AIBN)
* **Solvent:** Acetonitrile

## Synthesis & Deposition Steps
1. **Mixing:** Mix ATA, MAA, EGDMA, and AIBN in 10 mL of acetonitrile.
2. **Degassing:** Degas the solution with N₂ gas for 5 minutes.
3. **Polymerization:** Seal and polymerize under UV light for 6 hours (or thermally at 60°C for 12 hours).
4. **Template Removal:** Crush the resulting polymer and wash with an acetic acid/ethanol (1:4) solution to extract the ATA, leaving empty recognition cavities behind.
5. **Deposition:** Dry the polymer powder, dissolve it in a small amount of acetonitrile, and drop-cast 5–10 µL directly onto the clean gold electrode surface of the 10 MHz QCM crystal.
